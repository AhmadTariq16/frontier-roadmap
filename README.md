# Frontier Lab Roadmap

An interactive study plan for going from full-stack data scientist to an AI/ML engineer at the level of a frontier AI lab.

**Live page:** https://ahmadtariq16.github.io/frontier-roadmap/

## What's inside

Ten phases, ordered so each one builds on the last, at about 15 hours a week over 18 to 24 months.

| # | Phase | Length |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Calibrate and sharpen the foundations | 3 to 5 weeks |
| 2 | Deep learning from first principles | 4 to 6 weeks |
| 3 | NLP and text representations | 5 to 7 weeks |
| 4 | Transformers and LLMs from scratch | 8 to 10 weeks |
| 5 | Multimodal and real-world data | 4 to 6 weeks |
| 6 | GPU performance and distributed training | 12 to 14 weeks |
| 7 | Post-training: SFT, preference learning and RL | 8 to 10 weeks |
| 8 | Inference and serving | 4 to 6 weeks |
| 9 | Evals, agents and harnesses | 4 to 6 weeks |
| 10 | Specialize and ship public work | 3 to 6 months |

Each phase includes:

- **Topics to master.** Each one expands into specific concepts with short explanations. Every concept name is a web search link.
- **Self-test questions.** Interview-style questions to check whether you understand a topic.
- **Key resources.** Courses, books, papers and repos.
- **A project** to build and publish.
- **"You're done when" checks** that track your progress.

The page also has specialization tracks, back-of-envelope numbers to memorize, a weekly study rhythm, and notes on getting hired.

## Using the page

- Use the search box above the phases to find any topic or concept, e.g. "KV cache" or "FSDP".
- Use **Expand all** and **Collapse all** to open or close every topic.
- Click a checkpoint on the loss curve at the top to jump to that phase.
- Checkmarks are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on the device and browser where you tick them, and they don't sync between devices.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole page. It's a single self-contained file with no build step; fonts load from Google Fonts.
- `.nojekyll`: tells GitHub Pages to serve the file as-is.

To view it offline, download `index.html` and open it in a browser.
